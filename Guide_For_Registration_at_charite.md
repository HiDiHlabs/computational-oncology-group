# Registration as a Medical Doctor (Dr. med.) at **Charité**

# 1. Overview

This brief guide is for those who want to register at **Charité** as a Medical Doctor (Dr. Med). To see the difference between a variety of doctoral tracks, please look up to this page.
[Registration: Promotionsbüro - Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin](https://promotion.charite.de/en/doctoral_procedure/regulations_2017/registration/)


# 2. Key Steps

**Note: Steps 2.1 and 2.2 can be completed before arriving in Berlin.**

## 2.1 ZAB Approval

- If your degrees were obtained outside Germany, **ZAB approval** is required for certification.
- The process takes **1 to 3 months**, so apply early to avoid delays in semester registration.
- **Document Submission:** Both the **original** and **English** versions are required. For example, place the original Bachelor's degree certificate on **page 1** and the English translation on **page 2**.
    
    ### **Required Documents:**
    
    1. Master’s Graduation Certificate
    2. Master’s Degree Certificate
    3. Master’s Transcripts
    4. Bachelor’s Graduation Certificate
    5. Bachelor’s Degree Certificate
    6. Bachelor’s Transcripts
    7. High School Graduation Certificate (optional)
    8. Final Examination Results from Upper Secondary School (optional)
    9. University Entrance Examination Results
    10. Passport
    11. Master’s Thesis & Defense Certificates/Grades (optional but recommended)
    
    Once all documents are prepared, contact Alexandra Friedrich, head of Project Management and Organisation team. [Alexandra.Friedrich@bih-charite.de](mailto:Alexandra.Friedrich@bih-charite.de) for submission to **ZAB**. You may also send them to ZAB directly by yourself, but this usually costs **€200**.
    

## 2.2 Registration of your Doctoral Project

1. **Complete the Online Form**
    - Fill in the doctoral project details on the website.[https://promotion.charite.de/en/doctoral_procedure/regulations_2017/registration/dr_med](https://promotion.charite.de/en/doctoral_procedure/regulations_2017/registration/dr_med)
    - The description does not need to be overly detailed, as your project may evolve over time.
2. **Supervisor Confirmation**
    - After submission, your primary supervisor will receive a confirmation email.
    - Kindly remind them to confirm your registration.
3. **Review Process**
    - The review can take a long time, even several months, so be patient. If there is no response after 6 weeks, please contact the Doctoral Office.
4. **Signing the Doctoral Agreement**
    - Once verified, the Doctoral Office will send the **doctoral agreement** for signatures.
    - You and all your supervisors need to sign it.
5. **Submit the Required Documents**
    - **Signed doctoral agreement**
    - **Medical examination certificate** (obtained after arrival)
    - **ZAB approval** (which is why early preparation is important)

---

## 2.3 After you are here

- Once you arrive in Berlin and join our group, since you are paid externally (via CSC or your Hospital), you would need a guest contract to access Charite resources and get a personal ID (e.g. email, HPC, etc). We will deal with this when you join.
- Once you get the personal ID, please reach out to project manager for assistance to make a reservation for your medical examination. NOTE: Please take your vaccination records and translate this into English version at a local International Travel Health Care Center ( a yellow paper).
- After your medical examination, you will receive a letter of the results, please scan them into pdf, combined with your signed doctoral agreement and the ZAB approval to the doctoral office.

## 2.4 After Registration, Enrollment.

- Once you finish all the above steps, the doctoral office will register your doctoral project and then send you the certification of your doctoral project registration.
- Then you need to go to this page for enrollment as a doctoral student at **Charité. This page contains step-by-step instructions for the enrollment.**
    - [Enrollment for a doctoral degree: Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin](https://www.charite.de/en/teaching_learning/application_admission/enrollment_for_a_doctoral_degree/)
- Again, fill up a online application form on the website, pay a registration fee about 320 euro, and then send all the required files to the  [**promotion-immatrikulation(at)charite.de](mailto:promotion-immatrikulation@charite.de).**
- Wait 3-5 days

## 2.5 Finally!

- After a few days, you will receive the enrollment certification for your doctoral study.



# **Advantages of Enrollment:**

- **Public Transport Savings**
    - Instead of paying **€58/month** for the **Deutschland Ticket**, you can use the **student semester ticket** at a lower cost (included in the registration fee).
- **Student Housing (Studentenwohnheim)**
    - You can apply for **public student apartments**, which have significantly lower rent (**€400–500/month**).
    - The waiting time is **even 18 months**, so the sooner you apply, the better.
- **Discounted Meals**
    - With your student card, you get **a student discount** at the Mensa compared to using a guest card.
- **Fulfilling the Enrollment Requirement for Graduation**
    - To graduate with a **Dr. Med.**, you must be enrolled for **at least 4 semesters**.
    - The sooner you enroll, the sooner you fulfill this requirement.

# **Why Not Hurry?**
While internal procedures within our institute usually progress quickly, steps involving the Charité Doctoral Office often take longer, particularly the review of the submitted online form. Unfortunately, these external delays are beyond our control.

And luckily once you sign the contract, you will have enough access to the computational and other resources within the BIH for the daily works.
